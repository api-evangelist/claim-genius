# Claim Genius

> AI vehicle-inspection and automated damage-assessment company for auto insurance claims,
> underwriting, dealerships, salvage, lease/rental, fleet and collision repair. Its GeniusINSPECT
> engine turns vehicle photos and video into structured, decision-ready data: per-part damage
> classification, repair-versus-replace decisions, labor and paint hours, OEM part pricing,
> repairability status, ACV and total-loss probability. Operating across the US, EU and Asia since
> 2018. Integration is via the GeniusAPI Suite, a JWT-authenticated REST API with asynchronous
> pipeline processing.

## Access

Claim Genius publishes no public API reference and no machine-readable specification. The homepage
advertises an OpenAPI 3.0 spec and native iOS/Android capture SDKs; neither is available at a public
URL. Developer access and documentation are requested by email at contactus@claimgenius.com. A
provisioned ReadMe developer hub exists at https://claimgenius.readme.io but is password-protected
(HTTP 401).

## Products

- [GeniusINSPECT for Claims](https://claimgenius.com/geniusclaim): AI-driven damage detection, repairability and total-loss scoring for claims.
- [GeniusINSPECT for Underwriting](https://claimgenius.com/geniuspreinspect): Instant risk and damage scoring for underwriting and pre-bind vehicle inspection.
- [GeniusPrepost](https://claimgenius.com/geniusinspect-prepost): Comparison of vehicle damage between two points in time (pre- and post-repair, check-in/check-out).
- [GeniusOPS](https://claimgenius.com/geniusops): Human-in-the-loop operations — certified repair experts review, validate and approve AI output; AI-enabled TPA services.
- [GeniusAPI Suite](https://claimgenius.com/geniusapi): The REST integration layer connecting all Genius products through a single authenticated surface.

## APIs

- [GeniusAPI Suite](https://claimgenius.com/geniusapi): JWT-authenticated REST API. Documented flow — generate a token at `/api/auth/token` with an API ID and secret; upload JPG/PNG/MP4/MOV to `/api/file/upload`; POST claim metadata to `/api/pipelines/{pipelineName}` to receive a queue ID; poll `/api/pipeline/requests/{requestId}` for structured JSON results and signed media URLs. Vehicle make/model/variant and vehicle-code lookup endpoints normalize VIN inputs. Pipeline paths are date-versioned (e.g. `/api/pipelines/202103/...`). No public base URL is documented.
